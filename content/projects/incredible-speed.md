Incredible Speed is a SNES-inspired superhero theme by Partials set to a wonderfully dystopian gif loop by [Waneella](https://www.waneella.com/).

The track was originally written by Alex Eversbusch, played by partials (I played guitar, sang backup vocals, and helped with production), mixed by Drew Vandenberg, and mastered by Joe Lambert.

I am responsible for all of the video editing on top of Waneella’s original pixel art gif.
