I teach private music lessons to all ages online or in person in the Athens, Georgia area. I am certified with a Bachelors of Music in Music Education from the University of Georgia and have been teaching privately for over 10 years.

### Lessons Offered:

- All Band and Orchestra instruments (Woodwinds, Brass, Percussion, Strings)
- Early Childhood Music (ages 5-10)
- Guitar (Classical, Acoustic, Electric)
- Bass Guitar
- Drums
- Singing
- Improvisation, Songwriting
- Recording/Production, Sound Design (Hardware & Software Synthesis)
