I got my start in the formal music education track at the University of Georgia studying classical saxophone under [Dr. Connie Frigo](https://centerstage.conn-selmer.com/artists/connie-frigo), jazz under [Steve Dancz](https://stevedancz.com/) and [Dave DiAngelo](https://www.music.uga.edu/directory/people/david-dangelo), conducting: Dr. John Lynch and music therapy under Dr. John Kennedy and Dr. Ellen Richie.

![[diploma.jpg]]

Ey I have a degree! 😀

Just to name drop more credentials, I also marched for 8 years. 

- Bari Sax, then Drum Major conducting the Kennesaw Mountain High School Marching Band at the Bands of America Grand National Championship. Twice.
- Tenor Sax section leader of the UGA Redcoat Marching Band 2010-2013
- Dixieland Soloist 2009-2013
- Member of The Derbies, the elite pep band for away games
- Drum set player for the UGA Volleyball Band and Basketball Band
