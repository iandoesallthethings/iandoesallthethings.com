A toy-box of analog and digital video tools, many of which are built mostly from open-source projects.

![[PXL_20221229_174900854.jpg]]

Taken in the middle of a rebuild. I’ll get another shot once it’s finished.

### Video Sources

- [Critter & Guitari Eyesy](https://www.critterandguitari.com/eyesy) - Hackable, python powered digital video synth
- R_E_C_U_R - a raspberry pi video player/looper with a shader engine
- [CHA/V](https://jonasbers.com/chav/) - An analog video synth based on a VGA test signal generator
- Some old security cameras and drone cameras
- Apple TV + Ipad - Using a cobbled selection of apps from youtube to TouchViz to Eran Hileli’s Looom.

### Boring Stuff:

- Edirol V-4 video mixer
- Marshall V-R44P rack monitors
- Tascam PB-32R Rca Patchbay
- ProX 10U mixer case
