In 2020, [Josiah Garrett](https://josiahsorengarrett.com/) assembled a dream team of funk, jazz, pop, and gospel musicians from the Athens and Atlanta music scene, including members of [Universal Sigh](https://www.universalsighmusic.com/), [The Funk Brotherhood](http://www.thefunkbrotherhood.com/), [Fooshee’s Forecast](https://www.foosheesforecast.com/), [list more bands plz], and of course, Me. From <a href=partials>Partials</a>.

Over the course of several recording sessions, Josiah Soren & the Colorblind Pilots landed on a punchy collection of tunes with a variety of sounds.

I recorded on the hornline playing Baritone and Tenor saxophone and had a hand in writing the horn charts.
