A gaggle of about 60 Athens GA musicians were dropped into a hat and picked into groups of 3. Almost none of the groups had ever played together before.

3 musicians, 1 month, 10-15 minute set. And this is what we landed on! Luckily, we had the <a href=modular>Rhizome</a> to guide us and two entire rehearsals to cobble the show together.
