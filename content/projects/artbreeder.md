# What is it?

Originally hacked together in a weekend by [Joel Simon of Morphogen](http://morphogen.io), [Artbreeder](http://artbreeder.com) aims to be a new type of creative tool that empowers users’ creativity by making it easier to collaborate and explore. It started as Ganbreeder, an experiment in using breeding and collaboration as methods of exploring high complexity spaces. Artbreeder is named after the research of [Picbreeder](http://picbreeder.org/) which investigated the role of exploration in the optimization process. It is also inspired by an earlier project of Joel’s, [Facebook Graffiti](http://www.joelsimon.net/facebook-graffiti.html), which demonstrated the creative capacity of crowds.

Now Artbreeder has evolved far beyond its humble GAN origins to make use of the most cutting edge diffusion and embedding techniques. It’s growing into an ecosystem of tools that can seamlessly pass assets back and forth. Many of the tools can arbitrarily mix image and text prompts, resulting in one of the most flexible, accessible entry points to AI art tools on the market.
